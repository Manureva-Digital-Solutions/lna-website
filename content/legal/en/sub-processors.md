LNA is published by Lemon Tree SAS. To operate the service, Lemon Tree uses sub-processors — service providers that process personal data on Lemon Tree's behalf, under written contracts that impose data protection obligations equivalent to those in our [Data Processing Agreement](/en/dpa).

This page does not publish a named list of those providers. The current list, including each provider's activity and location, is available to customers on request, as described below.

<h2 id="1-role-of-sub-processors">1. Role of sub-processors</h2>

A sub-processor is engaged only as needed to provide LNA, for example for hosting, extraction, payments, or transactional email. Lemon Tree remains responsible to the Customer for each sub-processor's performance of its data-protection obligations.

<h2 id="2-general-written-authorisation">2. General written authorisation</h2>

By accepting the [Terms of Service](/en/terms) and the [Data Processing Agreement](/en/dpa), the Customer gives Lemon Tree a general written authorisation, within the meaning of Article 28(2) GDPR, to engage sub-processors to process Customer Data.

<h2 id="3-prior-notice-and-right-to-object">3. Prior notice and right to object</h2>

Lemon Tree informs the Customer of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor at least 30 days before the change takes effect, by email to the account administrator.

The Customer may object to the change on reasonable data protection grounds within 15 days of the notice. The parties then discuss the objection in good faith. If it is not resolved, the Customer may terminate the affected service before the change takes effect, and receives a pro-rata refund of any prepaid fees for the unused period.

<h2 id="4-how-to-obtain-the-current-list">4. How to obtain the current list</h2>

The current list of sub-processors, with their activity and location, is available to any Customer on request at [privacy@lnaflow.com](mailto:privacy@lnaflow.com). Lemon Tree responds within 15 days of the request.
