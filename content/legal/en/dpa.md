<h2 id="1-parties-and-scope">1. Parties and scope</h2>

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between:

- **the Customer**, any organisation that has subscribed to LNA under the [Terms of Service](/en/terms) ("Customer"); and
- **Lemon Tree SAS**, a French simplified joint-stock company (SAS) with share capital of €2,500, whose registered office is at 21 rue du Docteur Fournier, 37000 Tours, France, registered with the RCS Tours under SIREN 904 401 882, publisher and seller of LNA ("Lemon Tree", "we").

This DPA applies to **all Customers**, whatever their plan. It forms part of the Terms of Service and is accepted together with them. It applies whenever Lemon Tree processes personal data on the Customer's behalf through LNA.

<h2 id="2-definitions">2. Definitions</h2>

"Personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data breach" and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given in Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"). "Customer Data" means the personal data contained in the documents the Customer uploads to LNA and in the data LNA extracts and exports from them. "Sub-processor" means any processor engaged by Lemon Tree to process Customer Data.

<h2 id="3-roles-of-the-parties">3. Roles of the parties</h2>

- For Customer Data, the Customer is the **controller** and Lemon Tree SAS is the **processor**.
- For account and billing data (such as users' names and email addresses, and subscription and payment data), Lemon Tree acts as an independent **controller**, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy). That processing is outside the scope of this DPA.

<h2 id="4-subject-matter-nature-purpose-and-duration">4. Subject matter, nature, purpose and duration</h2>

Lemon Tree processes Customer Data only to provide LNA: storing uploaded documents, extracting data with AI, mapping it into the Customer's Excel templates, and exporting it, together with related features such as questions and answers on the documents. The processing lasts for the term of the Customer's subscription, plus the deletion period in section 12. The details are in Annex 1.

<h2 id="5-documented-instructions-art-28-3-a">5. Documented instructions (Art. 28(3)(a))</h2>

- Lemon Tree processes Customer Data only on the Customer's documented instructions. These are the Terms of Service, this DPA, and the Customer's use and configuration of LNA. This includes transfers to a third country, which take place only under section 9. The exception is processing required by EU or Member State law, in which case Lemon Tree informs the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it.
- Lemon Tree informs the Customer immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other data protection law.
- Lemon Tree does not use Customer Data to train AI models and does not sell it.
- The Customer must not upload special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR, including health data. Such data is not accepted on LNA and is not sent to the AI model provider (Mistral AI). A corresponding clause is proposed for the Terms of Service.

<h2 id="6-confidentiality-art-28-3-b">6. Confidentiality (Art. 28(3)(b))</h2>

Lemon Tree ensures that the people authorised to process Customer Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate legal duty of confidentiality. [TO CONFIRM — the form of that commitment (employment contract clause, NDA).]

<h2 id="7-security-of-processing-art-28-3-c-art-32">7. Security of processing (Art. 28(3)(c), Art. 32)</h2>

Lemon Tree implements the technical and organisational measures described in Annex 2, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation and the risks to data subjects. Lemon Tree may update these measures as long as the overall level of protection is not reduced.

<h2 id="8-sub-processors-art-28-2-3-d-and-4">8. Sub-processors (Art. 28(2), (3)(d) and (4))</h2>

- **General written authorisation.** The Customer gives Lemon Tree a general written authorisation, within the meaning of Article 28(2) GDPR, to engage sub-processors to process Customer Data.
- **Current list.** The list of sub-processors currently engaged, with their activity and location, is available to the Customer on request at [privacy@lnaflow.com](mailto:privacy@lnaflow.com). Lemon Tree responds within 15 days of the request.
- **Prior notice of changes.** Lemon Tree informs the Customer of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor at least 30 days before the change takes effect, by email to the account administrator.
- **Right to object.** The Customer may object to the change on reasonable data protection grounds within 15 days of the notice. The parties then discuss the objection in good faith. If it isn't resolved, the Customer may terminate the affected service before the change takes effect, and receives a pro-rata refund of any prepaid fees for the unused period.
- **Flow-down and liability.** Lemon Tree engages each sub-processor under a written contract that imposes data protection obligations equivalent to those in this DPA, including sufficient guarantees for appropriate technical and organisational measures. Lemon Tree remains fully liable to the Customer for each sub-processor's performance of its obligations (Art. 28(4)).

<h2 id="9-international-transfers-art-44-46">9. International transfers (Art. 44–46)</h2>

- Customer Data is hosted in the European Union, in France, on a VPS operated by OVH.
- Lemon Tree, or a sub-processor, transfers Customer Data to a country outside the European Economic Area only if the transfer complies with Chapter V GDPR, through an adequacy decision (Art. 45) or appropriate safeguards (Art. 46), in particular the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission (Decision (EU) 2021/914) together with supplementary measures where needed.
- Customer Data is currently accessed from **India** for operating and supporting the platform. Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module 3, processor to processor) **are being put in place** with the Indian entity that accesses the data, together with access controls. Those SCCs are **not yet signed**. Until they are signed, this transfer is not covered by an Article 46 safeguard.
- The AI model provider used for extraction is **Mistral AI** (Mistral AI, a French company, 15 rue des Halles, 75001 Paris). Mistral's help centre states that data is hosted in the EEA by default, but the global API endpoint (`api.mistral.ai`) does not commit to a specific inference location. Some Mistral features may temporarily transfer data outside the EU, under Mistral's own SCCs.

<h2 id="10-assistance-to-the-customer-art-28-3-e-and-f">10. Assistance to the Customer (Art. 28(3)(e) and (f))</h2>

- Taking into account the nature of the processing, Lemon Tree helps the Customer with appropriate technical and organisational measures to respond to data subjects' requests to exercise their rights. Customers can export or delete their data at any time from their account settings. Lemon Tree forwards to the Customer, without undue delay, any request it receives directly from a data subject about Customer Data.
- Lemon Tree helps the Customer meet its obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation), taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to Lemon Tree. [TO CONFIRM — whether this assistance is free or charged.]

<h2 id="11-personal-data-breaches-art-33-2">11. Personal data breaches (Art. 33(2))</h2>

Lemon Tree notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data, and in any case within 48 hours. The notice goes to the account administrator's email. It includes the information available at the time, as listed in Article 33(3) GDPR, and is completed as further information becomes available.

<h2 id="12-deletion-or-return-of-data-art-28-3-g">12. Deletion or return of data (Art. 28(3)(g))</h2>

- Throughout the subscription the Customer can export its documents and extracted data.
- When the services end, Lemon Tree deletes Customer Data within 30 days, unless EU or Member State law requires it to be kept. [TO CONFIRM — how long backup copies persist, and whether a deletion certificate is provided on request.]

<h2 id="13-audits-and-information-art-28-3-h">13. Audits and information (Art. 28(3)(h))</h2>

Lemon Tree makes available to the Customer all information needed to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR, and allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or by an auditor it mandates. The Customer may conduct one audit per twelve-month period, on at least 30 days' written notice, during business hours and without unreasonably disrupting operations. Lemon Tree first makes relevant documentation available. The auditor is bound by confidentiality. The Customer bears its own audit costs, unless the audit reveals a material non-compliance with this DPA, in which case Lemon Tree bears the reasonable costs of that audit.

<h2 id="14-liability">14. Liability</h2>

The limitation of liability in section 9 of the Terms of Service also applies to this DPA.

<h2 id="15-term-precedence-language-and-governing-law">15. Term, precedence, language and governing law</h2>

- This DPA stays in force for as long as Lemon Tree processes Customer Data on the Customer's behalf.
- If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Service on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails.
- **Language.** This DPA is available in French and in English. If the two versions conflict or differ in interpretation, the French version prevails.
- This DPA is governed by French law. Disputes are subject to the courts of Paris, France.
